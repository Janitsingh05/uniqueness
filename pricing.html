How credits work

  • Captioning is charged by the minute of source video. A 45-second clip costs 45 seconds.
  • Re-styling, re-timing and re-exporting the same clip is free — you pay once per minute of source video.
  • One-time pack credits never expire. Monthly plan minutes reset each cycle and do not roll over.
  • If a render fails, its credit comes straight back to your account.
